## 2025-05-14 - Initial Assessment
**Learning:** The Madison Site Surveyor app has a clean, dark industrial UI but lacks some critical micro-UX elements like confirmation for destructive actions (Clear All) and accessible labeling for icon-only buttons. The history view also presents a dead-end when empty.
**Action:** Implement confirmation for "Clear All", add ARIA labels to shutter and refresh buttons, and provide a clear CTA in the empty history state.
